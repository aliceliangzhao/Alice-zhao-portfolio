AWS S3 Tables: 0 → 1 in 8 weeks

  • Design lead
  • 2024
  • From concept to launch
  • Cloud infrastructure

Customers were spending millions building and maintaining complex infrastructure for AI/ML workloads. I led a 20-person team to design S3 Tables, a new product to eliminate that complexity, from concept to launch in 8 weeks.

S3 Tables, featured in the 2024 AWS CEO keynote, handles infrastructure automatically, providing a seamless console experience that lets teams turn big data into insights in seconds.

01Who and why

20+ interviews revealed that customers don't want to store structured data in unstructured storage while maintaining custom infrastructure. They need a solution to streamline storage operations so teams can focus on data, querying, and insight.

Data engineers maintain storage systems daily. Strategic leads evaluate infrastructure costs and reliability.

About

I am a product designer focused on the dense, high-stakes surfaces teams rely on all day: consoles, data tools, and the design systems that hold them together.

My work pairs systems thinking with a bias for shipping. I like taking ambiguous, technical problems from zero to one, then refining them into durable patterns other teams can build on.
